== Key Differences from Boxer hal.h ==
1. Sticks: ADC pots (PA0-PA3), NOT Flysky Hall serial
2. I2C1: PB8/PB9 (not PB6/PB7)
3. LCD: A0=PD12, RST=PC11 (Boxer has A0=PC11, RST=PD12 — SWAPPED)
4. PA9: Software BOOT0 control (Boxer: USB_VBUS)
5. No SD card detect pin (PD9 is NC)
